# Pausing with Marks instead of Pomodoros that can't be split

The book treats a Pomodoro as something that can't be split: any interruption voids it. We allow Pomodoros to be Paused and resumed instead. The pause isn't hidden: it leaves a Pause Mark, and the same pattern covers other bent rules (Overrun Mark, Skipped-Break Mark). Marks are only for information and never change what counts. We chose this because real life interrupts you, and throwing away 20 minutes of work does more harm than good. Keeping an honest record keeps you honest without punishing you. This is deliberate, not a mistake to "fix" back to the book's version.

## Considered Options

- **Strict (the book's rule)**: no pausing, and any stop voids the Pomodoro. Rejected as too punishing.
- **Pause without a trace**: rejected because the app would then be just another timer.
- **Interruption tallies (`'` internal, `-` external)**: the book's way of protecting a Pomodoro without stopping it. Left out for now. Pausing is the only way to handle an interruption.
