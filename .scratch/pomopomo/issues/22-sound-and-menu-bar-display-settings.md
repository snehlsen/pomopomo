# 22: Sound and menu-bar display settings

**What to build:** The "Glass" sound always plays and can't be muted, and the menu bar countdown ticks every second, although the method discourages watching the clock. Add settings to turn the sound off and to choose how the menu bar shows time: minutes and seconds, minutes only, or icon only.

**Blocked by:** 25 (settings window)

**Status:** done

- [ ] The completion sound can be turned off. It's on by default.
- [ ] The menu bar display can be set to minutes and seconds (default), minutes only, or icon only.
- [ ] Minutes only never shows 0 minutes while time is left, matching how the countdown rounds up.
- [ ] Both settings survive relaunching the app.
