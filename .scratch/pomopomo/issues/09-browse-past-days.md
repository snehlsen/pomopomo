# 09: Browsing past Days

**What to build:** You can go back through previous Days and see each one exactly as it was left: its Tasks with their Estimates and whether they're Done, and each Pomodoro with its state and Marks. Past Days are read-only.

**Blocked by:** 08 (day rollover)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] You can move back and forward between Days, and jump back to today.
- [ ] A past Day shows its Tasks, their Estimates and whether each is Done.
- [ ] A past Day shows every Pomodoro's state (Completed, Voided, or Paused for good) and its Marks.
- [ ] Nothing can be edited on a past Day.
