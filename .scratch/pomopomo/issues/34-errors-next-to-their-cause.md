# 34: Errors next to their cause

**What to build:** Error messages show in red at the bottom of the popover, far from what caused them, and stay until the next successful action. Show them next to the relevant control, and clear them automatically. Where possible, prevent the error in the UI instead, so the message never needs to appear.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately)

**Status:** todo

- [ ] An error appears near the control that caused it.
- [ ] Errors clear on their own after a few seconds or on the next interaction.
- [ ] Saving errors, which have no single control, still show somewhere clearly visible and don't clear until saving works again.
