# 18: Past Day summary

**What to build:** When browsing a past Day, show a one-line summary at the top, e.g. "8 Completed · 1 Voided · 3 Marks · 4/5 Tasks Done". Reviewing how a Day went is most of the reason to look back.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately)

**Status:** todo

- [ ] A past Day shows how many Pomodoros were Completed and Voided.
- [ ] It shows how many Marks were given, ideally split by kind.
- [ ] It shows how many Tasks were Done out of the Day's total.
- [ ] Pomodoros left Paused for good are counted separately or clearly left out.
- [ ] The summary comes from the core, and automated tests cover it.
